Why Gutter Cleaning is Essential for Austin Homes

Austin weather brings everything from spring pollen to fall leaves and sudden rainstorms. Without proper gutter maintenance, clogs and overflow can cause serious problems:

  • Roof leaks and fascia rot

  • Foundation erosion and mold growth

  • Pest nesting in stagnant water

  • Water stains on siding and brick

Regular gutter cleaning helps protect your home’s structure and improves curb appeal. AirPro Austin provides thorough service using safe ladder systems, pressure flushing tools, and debris-containment methods to keep your property spotless.

Our Gutter Cleaning & Repair Process

We combine attention to detail with professional equipment for reliable results every time.

Step-by-Step Service

  • Inspection: We start with a full roofline and downspout inspection to assess buildup, damage, and drainage flow.

  • Cleaning: All leaves, dirt, and debris are hand-removed and flushed through downspouts.

  • Repairs: We seal minor leaks, tighten loose fasteners, and replace damaged sections if needed.

  • Testing: After cleaning, gutters are water-tested to ensure proper drainage.

Our technicians always provide before-and-after photos and recommendations for ongoing maintenance or new installation if your gutters are outdated.
