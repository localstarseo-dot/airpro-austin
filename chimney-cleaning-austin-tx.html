Warning Signs

Signs Your Chimney Needs Attention

Some concerns call for cleaning. Others need a closer inspection before you use the fireplace again.

Visible Soot or Dark Deposits

Deposits around the fireplace may be a reason to have the chimney checked.

Persistent Fireplace Odors

Deposits, moisture, or airflow problems can cause odors. An inspection can help identify the source.

Smoke Coming Into the Room

Stop using the fireplace and arrange a professional assessment before using it again.

Unsure When It Was Last Inspected?

An inspection can help establish the chimney’s condition before further use.

Chimney Cleaning FAQs

How Often Should I Have My Chimney Inspected or Cleaned?

The Chimney Safety Institute of America recommends a yearly inspection and cleaning when needed. How often you need sweeping depends on use, deposits, and the chimney’s condition. Read CSIA’s homeowner guidance .

Does Chimney Cleaning Include an Inspection?

Your quote should explain what inspection is included. Ask whether camera checks, written reports, or more detailed inspections are included or priced separately.
