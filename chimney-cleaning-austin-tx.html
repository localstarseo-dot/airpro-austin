Chimney Cleaning FAQs

How Much Does Chimney Cleaning Cost?

Request a quote for your chimney and fireplace rather than assuming a standard package price. Ask which cleaning and inspection work is included and what may cost extra. Contact AirPro Austin to discuss your service needs.

How Often Should a Chimney Be Inspected or Cleaned?

The Chimney Safety Institute of America recommends an inspection at least once a year, with cleaning when needed. Cleaning frequency depends on use, deposits, and condition. Read the CSIA homeowner guidance.

Can Chemical Chimney Cleaners Replace Professional Sweeping?

No. Chemical cleaning products are not a substitute for an inspection and mechanical cleaning when needed. Loosened debris may still need removal. CSIA explains the limitations of chemical chimney cleaners.

Does a Chimney Need Inspection If I Rarely Use It?

Yes. Limited use does not rule out nests, blockages, or deterioration. An inspection checks conditions that may not be obvious from the fireplace opening. CSIA’s homeowner FAQs explain why annual checks still matter.

Does a Gas Fireplace Need Professional Inspection?

Yes. Gas-burning equipment and its venting still need appropriate maintenance. The Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends yearly professional inspections of fuel-burning heating systems. Tell us your fireplace type when you contact our team so service suitability can be confirmed.

How Far Should Combustible Items Be Kept From a Fireplace?

Keep items that can burn at least three feet from heat sources, including fireplaces. Follow any greater clearances required by your equipment. The U.S. Fire Administration’s heating-safety guidance explains this precaution.

Does Chimney Cleaning Include an Inspection?

Our service page describes an inspection as part of the cleaning process. Before booking, confirm the inspection scope, including whether camera work or a written report is included. Do not assume a particular inspection level from the word “inspection” alone. AirPro Austin
