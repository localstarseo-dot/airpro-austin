UV Lights, Filters, and Duct Cleaning Do Different Jobs

Filters capture airborne particles, while cleaning removes buildup from the areas being serviced. A UV system has a separate purpose that depends on its design and where it is installed.

Some products target exposed HVAC surfaces, such as cooling coils. Others are designed to treat moving air. Ask which application the proposed model supports and what maintenance it needs.

HVAC UV Light Installation FAQs

What Does an HVAC UV Light Do?

Its purpose depends on the product. Some systems target exposed HVAC surfaces, while others are designed for air treatment. Ask what the proposed model is intended to do.

Will a UV Light Replace Air Filters or Duct Cleaning?

UV treatment does not physically remove dust and debris. Keep suitable filters and any necessary cleaning as separate parts of system care.

Can a UV Light Be Added to My Existing HVAC System?

Suitability depends on the equipment and installation requirements. Have the proposed product checked against your system before installation.

How Often Does the UV Lamp Need Replacing?

Follow the replacement schedule for the specific product. Ask about lamp life, maintenance, and replacement cost when reviewing the quote.
